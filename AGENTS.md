# BashMineralResource site guidance

## Brand

Industrial supplier of manganese-bearing flux, gypsum and anhydrite stone from Bashkortostan. The site should feel material, grounded and technically credible.

## Audience

Metallurgical buyers, production technologists, procurement teams and construction-material manufacturers who need composition, documents, volume and logistics clarity before a call.

## Tone

Precise, calm and practical. Use specific industrial language: quarry, crushing, fraction, passport, lab protocol, shipment, rail spur, trial batch.

## Style

Editorial industrial luxury: warm limestone, graphite, muted umber and one restrained wine accent. Prefer asymmetric layouts, large quarry imagery, premium surfaces, tabular figures and quiet motion.

## Avoid

Generic SaaS gradients, decorative blobs, repetitive equal card rows, placeholder article sections, loud sales copy, dead `#` CTAs, cramped mobile spacing and long navigation that wraps in the header.

## Primary CTA

Request a batch calculation or composition documents.

## Key Pages

Home narrative, product detail routes, product passport sections, document modal, quarry/gallery section and contact request form.
